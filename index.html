About me · The person behind the art

I go by Heart, and you can find me online as KHeart67. I’m an aspiring 2D and 3D animator and character designer who enjoys drawing, music, and visual storytelling. Portraits and original characters give me a way to explore feelings, personalities, and worlds that exist in my imagination.

I’m still building my skills through drawing projects, personal artwork, and hands-on practice. I’m drawn to goth and alternative self-expression, and the pink, purple, and black in this space reflect that side of me.

This website is both my creative portfolio and a record of my self-development. Alongside my artworks and characters, it shows what I’m learning about my strengths, the habits I’m working on, and the kind of person and artist I want to become.

My life mission & vision

My mission

To keep learning about myself while developing my drawing, animation, and character design skills through practice, experimentation, and feedback. I want to express myself honestly, treat people with kindness and fairness, and build habits that help me follow through on what matters to me.

In everyday life, this means setting manageable goals, making time to practice, and speaking up when I need help or have something to contribute.

My vision

To grow into a 2D and 3D animator and character designer who creates expressive characters and meaningful visual stories with other creative people. Beyond a career, I want to become more confident, consistent, and comfortable being myself.

I want a life where creativity and music have a place, where I can keep growing, and where I can support others without losing my own voice.

My strengths & weaknesses · Understanding myself

My strengths map connects patterns from my personality results, learning-style inventory, and character strengths. Looking at them together helps me understand why some approaches feel natural to me and why other parts of creative work need more support.

✧ Creative explorer

Openness, curiosity, and appreciation of beauty connect with my interest in art, music, and visual storytelling. They help me explore ideas and imagine characters, though I still need structure to turn those ideas into finished work.

♡ Compassionate collaborator

Kindness, fairness, honesty, and teamwork remind me to consider other people’s perspectives. These strengths matter when I listen, give feedback, or work with others. I’m also learning that being considerate includes speaking up about my own needs.

☾ Thoughtful visual learner

My learning-style results connect with how much images, demonstrations, and hands-on practice help me. Seeing a process and trying it myself gives me a clearer starting point. I can support this with visual plans and small practice exercises.

✿ Self-aware striver

I can participate and connect with people while also needing time to observe and work independently. Recognizing my difficulties with overthinking, confidence, and consistency helps me choose realistic ways to improve instead of expecting myself to be perfect.

Where I need support

I sometimes overthink my work, struggle to maintain motivation, or hesitate to speak up. These patterns can make starting, finishing, or sharing a project harder. They don’t erase my strengths, but they show me where I need a more supportive routine.

What the results helped me understand

Being creative and caring doesn’t automatically make me consistent or confident. My ideas need manageable deadlines, my learning needs examples and practice, and my confidence needs opportunities to ask questions and receive feedback. Understanding those connections gives me a clearer direction for growth.

Where I’m growing · My next steps

I’m working on overthinking, consistency, motivation, and speaking up. Small deadlines, visual plans, and finishable steps help me give my ideas a little more structure.

  • Consistency: choose one manageable drawing or animation exercise and make room for it in my week.
  • Overthinking: define a small goal for a piece, finish a draft, and choose one thing to improve next time.
  • Confidence: share a work in progress or ask for specific feedback instead of waiting until everything feels perfect.
  • Speaking up: practice asking questions, contributing ideas, and explaining what I need respectfully.

My goal is to support the creativity, care, and curiosity I already have with confidence and steady action. The life map in my study corner gives these intentions a place to begin.

My personal values & life philosophy

Creativity, kindness, fairness, and honesty matter to me. I want to make things that feel personal, respect different perspectives, and stay open to learning from others. I also value the freedom to express myself through art, music, and the way I present myself.

I want my work to leave room for emotion, personality, and imperfection. I don’t have to have everything figured out before I start making something meaningful.

For me, growing means becoming more comfortable with who I am while taking responsibility for the things I can improve. Every sketch and experiment is another chance to find my own voice.

My learning background

Drawing studies & creative practice

My learning includes Drawing 1 plates and a midterm drawing project, alongside personal sketches, portraits, and original character artwork. Working on both class projects and personal pieces gives me opportunities to explore ideas and practice completing work.

I also attend guitar workshops. Music is another part of how I express myself, and learning an instrument gives me another reason to practice patiently and keep trying.

I’m building toward 2D and 3D animation and character design through continued learning and creative exploration.
