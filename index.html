A little introduction

Hello, I’m Heart ♡

I’m an aspiring artist who enjoys drawing characters, exploring stories, listening to music, and finding ways to express myself through art.

My goal is to become a 2D and 3D animator and character designer. This portfolio is a place for my artwork, original characters, creative interests, and personal growth.

I’m still learning, experimenting, and trying to become more consistent. Some parts of this website are unfinished, just like some of my character stories.

My vision & mission

My vision

To become an animator and character designer who can turn ideas, emotions, and stories into meaningful visual work while staying true to myself.

My mission

To keep practicing my art, learn 2D and 3D animation, accept useful feedback, and build habits that help me finish projects instead of leaving every idea in my imagination.

My strengths & areas for growth

My strengths map connects my creativity, learning preferences, personal values, and the habits I want to improve.

Creative explorer

My openness and curiosity connect with my interest in art, music, character design, and visual storytelling. Exploring different ideas helps me develop my own creative voice.

Thoughtful visual learner

I understand ideas better through examples, images, observation, and hands-on practice. References and demonstrations help me turn an unfamiliar task into something I can try.

Compassionate collaborator

Fairness, kindness, honesty, and teamwork matter to me. I want to be considerate of other people and respectful of different perspectives.

Self-aware striver

I recognize my difficulties with overthinking, motivation, consistency, and speaking up. Understanding these patterns gives me a starting point for improving them.

What these patterns tell me

My creativity is supported by curiosity, careful judgment, and visual learning. My average Extraversion suggests that I can connect with others while also valuing time to observe, think, and work independently.

Being aware of my strengths does not mean pretending that my difficulties are gone. I need manageable deadlines, practice, feedback, and a routine that helps me act on my ideas.

My next steps: break projects into small tasks, ask for feedback earlier, practice regularly, and speak up when I need help or clearer boundaries.
My values & life philosophy

I value honesty, fairness, kindness, creativity, and the freedom to express myself. I want my work to feel like something I chose to make, while still being open to learning from others.

I don’t have to become exactly like everyone else to improve. I can grow, learn, and become more dependable without losing the things that make me myself.

Education & learning experiences

My current learning includes Drawing 1, art and design subjects, personal development, and other college coursework.

Drawing plates and projects help me practice observation, technique, and finishing work under deadlines. Guitar workshops also give me another way to develop my creativity through music.

More educational details and milestones can be added here later.
