A little about me

The person behind the art

I go by Heart, and you can find me online as KHeart67. Art and music are two of the ways I connect with ideas, feelings, and the world around me.

I enjoy visual storytelling, portraits, original characters, and exploring how color and movement can bring an idea to life. This space holds the work I’m making and the person I’m becoming along the way.

My mission & vision

My mission

To develop my drawing, animation, and character design skills through practice, experimentation, and feedback, while making work that feels honest to who I am.

My vision

To grow into a 2D and 3D animator and character designer who creates expressive characters and meaningful visual stories with other creative people.

✧ Creative explorer

♡ Compassionate collaborator

☾ Thoughtful visual learner

Where I’m growing

I’m working on overthinking, consistency, motivation, and speaking up. Small deadlines, visual plans, and finishable steps help me give my ideas more structure.

My goal is to support the creativity, care, and curiosity I already have with confidence and steady action.

My creative philosophy

I want my work to leave room for emotion, personality, and imperfection. I don’t have to have everything figured out before I start making something meaningful.

Learning to express myself is part of the process. Every sketch and experiment is another chance to find my own voice.

    Read Rion’s story · unfinished

    Rion is a dragon from the dragon world above. When a war between his family and a neighboring creature reached their home, he fell to the human land while still inside his egg.

    A human villager found the unusual egg and sold it to merchants. It passed through auctions before a nobleman bought it, and Rion eventually hatched in his possession. His owner ridiculed and abused him, then sold him at another auction as a slave. A higher-ranking nobleman bought him, but Rion escaped on the journey to his new owner’s house.

    He fled deep into the woods and wandered for days until he collapsed from hunger. There, he met Khianna, a pink-haired human girl. Her kindness and innocence convinced her father to take him in. Rion grew up with Khianna and her father for years, until Khianna turned sixteen.

    The human kingdom eventually discovered that a civilian was sheltering someone it considered a monster. The king ordered guards and villagers to find them and kill Rion. When they reached the house, the villagers threatened Khianna and her father with weapons and fire.

    Rion panicked and tried to protect them, but his reaction only intensified the villagers’ anger and hatred. He went on a rampage, breathing fire through the forest. Khianna and her father stepped back in terror as part of their burning house collapsed onto them, killing them both.

    When Rion saw Khianna burning, he lost control and killed the villagers. He believed they had killed her, unable to recognize that his own fire had caused her death.

    Afterward, he carried Khianna’s body and searched for a way to revive her, even if she could no longer return as a human.

    Lore is still unfinished. What happens next has not been decided.

    Khianna and her father gave Rion a home after he escaped captivity and collapsed from hunger in the woods. They lived together for years, until Khianna turned sixteen.

    When the kingdom discovered that they were sheltering Rion, villagers and guards came for him. During the confrontation, Rion’s fire spread, and part of the burning house collapsed onto Khianna and her father, killing them.
